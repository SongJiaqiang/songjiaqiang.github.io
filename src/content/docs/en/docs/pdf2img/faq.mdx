## Does it upload my PDFs?

Not to our servers. Page rendering runs on-device.

## Where do I download it?

Install it from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pdf2img/id1236146668). There is no separate installer.

## Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad, Apple silicon Macs, and Apple Vision. Minimum system is iOS / iPadOS 16.0.

## Can I export print quality?

Yes. Export is 300 DPI.
