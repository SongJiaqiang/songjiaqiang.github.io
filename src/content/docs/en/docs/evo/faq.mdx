## How is Evo different from EvoRadio?

[EvoRadio](https://github.com/SongJiaqiang/EvoRadio) is a 2016 Swift learning project. Evo is the 2020 Flutter rewrite, aimed at a simpler playback flow and modern phone screens.

## Is it still maintained?

It is a practice project, shown here as an archived app. The source remains on GitHub.

## Why does it look like Douban FM?

The rewrite borrowed Douban FM’s two-pane swipe and light listening flow to exercise Flutter layout and gestures.
