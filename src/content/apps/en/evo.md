Evo is a 2020 Flutter rewrite of [EvoRadio](https://github.com/SongJiaqiang/EvoRadio). The original iOS app had fallen behind — no notch support, fiddly playback — so the rewrite focused on a simpler daily player for ambient music.

The build log is in Chinese: [Flutter project notes — Evo](/blog/flutter-project-evo-01).

## What it tries to be

- Two-pane swipe between now playing and the station list
- A light Douban FM–like listening flow
- Background music you can actually live with

Source: [SongJiaqiang/Evo](https://github.com/SongJiaqiang/Evo). Getting started lives in the [docs](/en/docs/evo/).
